The Mood (Event Ticketing App)
DemoFeaturesScreenshotsGet it

Event ticketing that feels like the night itself.

The Mood sells tickets for concerts, comedy, conferences and nights out. Buyers find an event, pay by card and get QR e-tickets on their phone in under a minute. Organizers run events, sales, refunds, promo codes and the box office from one console, and the door team scans guests in from any phone.

What it doesFeatures

⌕Event discovery

  • Search with category, date and city filters
  • Featured events, "selling fast" and "sold out" badges
  • Event pages with live availability and a live price quote
  • Waitlist when an event sells out

₤Checkout & payments

  • Tickets held for 10 minutes while the buyer pays: no overselling
  • Real card payments with Stripe or Paymob
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay, mobile wallets and local methods
  • Promo codes, service fees and automatic refunds

▦QR e-tickets

  • One ticket per attendee, with a name on each
  • Print, save as PDF or add to calendar
  • "Find my tickets" by email and order number
  • A ticket page buyers can reopen any time

▤Organizer console

  • Dashboard: sales, tickets, check-ins and fees vs the previous period
  • Events, ticket types, sales windows and capacity
  • Orders with history, refunds and resends
  • Attendees with search and CSV export

✓Door check-in

  • Barcode scanners, typed codes or the phone camera
  • Big green / amber / red result for every scan
  • Second scans, refunded and wrong-event tickets caught
  • Each ticket admitted once, even across several doors

⚙Box office, team & brand

  • Door and phone sales (cash, card, transfer) and comps
  • Admin, Organizer and Door staff roles
  • Encrypted payment keys and signed webhooks
  • Your logo, colours and cover photos
